Hi Rahini,

I’m sorry for those words.

They were not mine. They never came from my heart, and they are nothing like the way I see you. I wish I could pull every single one of them back.

Relax, my princess. I won’t let your hands scroll this letter. Just sit back and let me talk to you for a minute.

This heart is only a quarter of my apology. It took me two hours to build, and I would happily spend two more, because you deserve so much more than this.

You walk into my thoughts without knocking and rearrange everything. Honestly, that’s a little unfair, because now I can’t think straight when you’re upset with me.

So here is my promise: gentler words, more patience, and a lot more of the version of me that makes you smile. (You have to admit I’m not bad at that.)

I miss your voice already, my Rin. Please forgive me, and let me make it up to you properly.

Tomorrow will be more surprises, my Rin.

Yours, hopefully,
Adarsh
